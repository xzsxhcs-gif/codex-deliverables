Review date: 2026-10-22. Scope: final `src/narration.json`, the source/asset ledgers, the original JPGs and their saved Commons metadata, `src/render.py` scene labels and credits, original illustration/music code, narration-generation code, and `assets/ATTRIBUTION.md`.

The root production agent performs the separate export/decode, frame and audio checks. A clean desk review is not a substitute for those checks.

## What was checked

- The script contains **237 words in 19 segments**, with limited place names and ordinary English rather than unsupported tourism superlatives or changing numerical claims.
- Yongkang is correctly described as a county-level city administered by Jinhua, in central Zhejiang and eastern China. The locator graphic is a clearly authored administrative hierarchy, not an invented geographic boundary map.
- The history sentence uses the **Three Kingdoms period / more than 1,700 years**, avoiding the exact founding year that the research ledger identifies as contested. The accompanying bridge photograph is labelled **later city heritage**, so it does not imply that Xijin Bridge dates from the Three Kingdoms.
- Product examples—power tools, household doors and kitchenware—have a suitable municipal source and are explicitly illustrated. The small hardware-market photograph is labelled as an **archival exterior**, not present-day factory production footage.
- The municipal tourism article directly supports Fangyan’s cliffs and Danxia landscape. The photographs are labelled Fangyan and not falsely described as a UNESCO World Heritage site.
- **Houwu, Huku, Zhiying and Xianghuli are not conflated.** The actual heritage shot uses the cleared Wu ancestral hall in Houwu. The official S13 page independently identifies Houwu as being in Yongkang and explicitly mentions an ancestral hall there. The Yanyi Hall photo is in the cleared asset pool but is not mislabeled as Houwu in the authored film.
- The temple-fair sentence is supported by a dated municipal report. Its still is labelled **Fangyan · scenic context**; the film does not claim it is a photograph of the fair, a particular 2026 performance, or proof of any visitor count.
- Roumaibing is described as a savory flatbread with pork and preserved vegetables. The ledger distinguishes the official local-standard catalogue from the newspaper ingredient reports and transparently marks the latter’s incomplete direct-page access. The food image is labelled **Original illustration**. It is not passed off as a documentary food photograph, and the script does not claim every recipe is identical.
- “Made by hand. Shaped by time.” and the other short headline phrases function as editorial motifs, while the narration itself explicitly describes a manufacturing economy. They should not be expanded into a claim that all local production is handmade.

## Photograph provenance and license review

The 16 cleared local source JPGs were independently rehashed and **all matched** their SHA-256 entries in `research/assets.json`. This confirms correspondence with the project’s cleared-file ledger, rather than proving independent original authorship.

For the repeatedly used `city-panorama` image, the abbreviated original title “永城” was not treated as location proof on its own: saved Commons metadata includes the Yongkang category and creation coordinates **28.846309, 120.045784**, consistent with the Yongkang area. Its date is explicitly an original upload date, not a verified shutter date.

The reviewed pool uses public domain, CC BY 2.0/3.0, and CC BY-SA 3.0/4.0 files. Per-shot credits name the listed author and license. `assets/ATTRIBUTION.md` preserves the original page, license link, modification description and source date for each file. Government/news pages are used as factual sources; their reserved-rights photographs have not been imported into the film.

The overall adaptation is offered as **CC BY-SA 4.0**, while the original images retain their individual terms. Creative Commons [BY-SA 3.0 legal code, section 4(b)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode.en) permits an adaptation under a later license version with the same license elements. Keep the complete credit file with the downloadable project, and keep an accessible link to it beside any standalone MP4 download. Do not describe the original public-domain or CC BY photos themselves as newly restricted to ShareAlike.

The opening and closing explicitly identify **archival photography**. Some metadata dates are capture dates and others are upload dates, so no blanket “shot in 2026” or precise capture-year range should be introduced in the final README or download description.
