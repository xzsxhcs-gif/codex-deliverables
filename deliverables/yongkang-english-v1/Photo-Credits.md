## Retrieval note

Commons API/CDN temporarily returned HTTP 429 during initial discovery. Broad inventory was stopped, requests were narrowed to selected files, a descriptive User-Agent with project contact URL was used, and Retry-After waits were respected. Metadata responses are preserved in `research/commons/*-metadata.json`.
